A coin that restores magicules when used.

## Information

| Type      | Item |
| --------- | ---- |
| Craftable | No   |

---

## Obtaining

The Coin can be obtained through the **Excavate** mode of the **Excavation** Unique Skill.

## Usage

Right-click to restore **250,000 magicules**. The Coin cannot raise the user's magicules above their maximum and is consumed on use.
