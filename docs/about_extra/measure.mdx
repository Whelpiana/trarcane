Allows the user to measure the distance between two selected blocks.

## Information

| Type | Extra Skill |
|------|-------------|
| Obtain Cost | 1000 MP |

---

## Obtaining

Measure is an Extra Skill that can be obtained through:

* Reaching 50,000 EP or higher.

---

## Usage

### [Active - Press] Measure

1. **Set Position 1**

   * Look at a block.
   * Hold **Shift** and press the skill.
   * The targeted block is saved as Position 1.

2. **Set Position 2**

   * Look at another block.
   * Press the skill normally.
   * The targeted block is saved as Position 2.

3. **Distance Calculation**

   * The skill calculates the distance between the two selected positions.
   * The result is displayed in chat in blocks.

### Target Range

* Maximum range: **50 blocks**

### Measurement

The distance is calculated using the largest difference between the X, Y, and Z coordinates of the two positions.
