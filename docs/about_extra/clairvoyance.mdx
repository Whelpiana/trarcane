title: Clairvoyance
icon: skills:clairvoyance
---

Reveals the location of a player through an Empty Element Core renamed to their name.

## Information

| Type | Extra Skill |
|------|-------------|
| Obtain Cost | 1000 MP |

---

## Obtaining

Clairvoyance is an Extra Skill that can be obtained through:

- Reaching 200,000 EP or higher.

---

## Usage

### [Active - Press] Clairvoyance

1. Rename an **Empty Element Core** to the name of a player who is currently online.
2. Hold the core in hand and press the mode. The core's lore will show the named player's dimension and coordinates at that moment.
